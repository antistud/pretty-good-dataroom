# Setting up the room and inviting people

## Who can be in the room

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Founder** | Everything, including settings and removing people |
| **Team** | Help run the room and the raise |
| **Investor** | See what their firm's access level allows, ask for things, offer introductions |
| **Supporter** | Receive updates and see introduction requests. Nothing confidential |

Nobody can sign themselves up. Everyone arrives by invitation, and an invitation works once and expires after 14 days.

## Access levels

Every folder, document, page and update section has an audience. There are three investor levels to start with:

- **Teaser** for a first look,
- **Diligence** once a conversation is serious,
- **Closing** for the final documents.

Supporters sit below all three. You can rename the levels and choose which may download files under **Settings**.

Access belongs to the **firm**, not the person. An investor can invite their own colleagues, who get the same access and never more.

## Fill the room

Under **The room** you will find:

- **Overview** and **Team**, which most investors read first.
- **Documents**, with folders ready for a pre-seed raise. PDFs are watermarked with the viewer's email.
- **Cap table**, with shareholders, the option pool and SAFEs, and a view of what happens at a priced round.
- **Financials**.

### Vesting, and people who have left

On the cap table, open **Dates and vesting** for a holder to record when they started, how long their shares vest over (usually 48 months) and the cliff (usually 12).

If someone leaves, add the date under **Left on**. The cap table then counts only the shares that had vested on their last day, shows what went back to the company, and recalculates everyone's percentage. For example, a founder granted 4,800,000 shares on four-year vesting who leaves after 21 months keeps 2,100,000.

Vesting is worked out monthly with nothing before the cliff. If your agreement says something different (acceleration, a negotiated exit), leave the vesting fields blank and enter the number of shares the person actually kept.

### What is still missing

The room notices when what you have entered points to paperwork that is not there. A signed SAFE on the cap table with no signed copy, founders with no stock purchase or IP assignment agreement, an option pool with no plan, an open round with no deck: each appears under **Still to upload** on the Documents page, with the reason and a button that opens the right folder.

It works from document and file names, so name files plainly ("SAFE - Oak Ventures (signed).pdf"). If something does not apply to your company, mark it **Not needed**. It is a prompt based on common pre-seed diligence, not legal advice.

The fastest way to fill these is to [let your AI assistant read your documents](ai-connector.md).

## Plan the round

**Fundraising → Rounds → New round** asks what the money is for and works out how much to raise and at what cap from your own numbers, explaining what founders usually do at each step.

## Track investors

**Fundraising → Investors** holds everyone you are talking to. You can import a contact list from a CSV file. Each card tells you the one thing to do next. Moving a card forward raises that firm's access to match, so you do not manage permissions by hand.

## Invite people

Open an investor's card and send the invitation from there, or use **People → Access**. They get an email with a link, and from then on sign in with an emailed code.

Investors are never given a checklist. Their home page shows a letter from you, what is in the room, and one question: what do you need, and by when?

## Send updates

**Updates** holds your monthly update. Each section has its own audience, so supporters, early conversations and committed investors each see the right amount. You can preview it as any reader before sending, and the room reminds you until it is published.

## Optional extras

- **Authenticator app.** Under **Account**, add a second sign-in step. Worth doing for founders.
- **Agreement before entering.** Under **Settings**, ask investors and supporters to accept a confidentiality text first. This is a click-through acknowledgement, not a signed contract.
- **Printing and export.** Every investor-facing page prints cleanly or saves as PDF. Levels allowed to download can also export the cap table as CSV and all documents as one zip.
